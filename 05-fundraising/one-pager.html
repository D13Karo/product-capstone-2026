The Problem

KIU students who play informal campus sports keep missing games — schedule changes get buried in noisy group chats across Messenger, Facebook and WhatsApp. 7 of 10 students we interviewed missed a match last semester; two campus sports groups collapsed from coordination fatigue.

The Solution

UniSport gives each campus one verified feed: an organiser posts a match once, every player at that university sees it, and any change pushes to everyone automatically — no reposting, no buried updates.
