The Solution

One screen replaces the chaos: the organiser posts a match once, and every change reaches every player automatically.

  • Organiser posts a match once — sport, time, venue, player limit.
  • Every verified player at that university sees it instantly.
  • Time moves, venue changes, game cancelled → an automatic push to everyone who joined.
  • No reposting across three apps. No checking five chats before you leave home.

One source of truth

One post. One push when anything changes. You always know where to be.

UniSportThe Solution

Why Now

Infrastructure shift

Free cross-platform push notifications (Expo Push) became available to small teams in early 2024. Before that, building this required paid infrastructure we couldn't afford.

Feed fragmentation

Facebook Groups quietly deprioritised organic posts since 2023, so the "official" announcement board became unreliable exactly when students needed it.

Our entry: we capture clear, time-sensitive intent precisely when the legacy channels lost viability.

Go-to-Market

One organiser brings their whole group — about 20 players. That's the leverage.

  • Channel 1 — organiser outreach (Messenger DM), ~₾1.6 per acquired user.
  • ~80% of an organiser's players follow them in.
  • First 500 users: 25 organisers × 4 campuses × ~20 players ≈ 500, for ~₾800 and 12 weeks.

Why it compounds

We acquire the supply side (organisers) directly and cheaply; the demand side (players) arrives for free with each organiser.
